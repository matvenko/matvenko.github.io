How to play

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

Every level is a new maze. Clear it, and the next one is a little bigger.

  1. 1

    Move

    Steer Boo with the on-screen joystick.

  2. 2

    Collect every coin

    Grab them all to clear the level. The mazes grow as you go deeper.

  3. 3

    Stay on solid ground

    Water, lava and quicksand are no place for a ghost. Fall off and you lose a life.

  4. 4

    Dodge the hunters

    One touch from a hunter costs a life. Keep moving and plan your way out.

Down to the last coin? A golden arrow shows the way.

Powers

A power for every close call

The right power at the right moment turns a close call into an escape.

Trap

Leave it behind you. The first hunter to step on it is locked in ice.

Freeze

Stop every hunter on the board at once.

Shield

For a few seconds, hunters and hazards can't hurt you.

Teleport

Jump to a safe spot far from the hunters.

Swipe

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Maze Boo free?

Yes. Maze Boo is free to download and play, and it has no in-app purchases. Ads are optional: a short video plays only when you choose one for a reward, such as an extra life.

What can I play it on?

On Android through Google Play, and on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) through the App Store. Maze Mode also runs in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the game and play. You get a place on the world leaderboard straight away, under a name the game makes up for you. Sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games to show your own name and keep your place on a new device.

Will I lose my progress if I close the game?

No. Your run saves as you play, so you can leave any time and pick up where you stopped.

How does Boo evolve?

Collect Boo Gems and open Boo's Journey from the main menu. The forms come in a fixed order, Frost Boo, then Guardian Boo, then Phantom Boo, and each one adds a new power. You keep every power you have unlocked, whichever form you play.

Which languages does it speak?

Maze Boo is in English.

I found a bug or need help.

Please write through the support page. You can read how the game handles data in the privacy policy.
